Satellite Fleet Operations

Twelve invented satellites, four ground stations, 18:00 on day one. One battery is warming, the passes cannot carry every request, debris is coming, and nothing is uplinked without two people. …
The fleet, its orbits, telemetry and the close approach are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real spacecraft. Orbits are circular two-body, not SGP4; the collision probability uses a stated uncertainty, not a covariance message. No outside model or service is called.
